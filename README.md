# 🗂️ Fintact — Personal Finance Tracker

A lightweight, private, single-file web app to track daily spending, allocate savings buckets, and manage personal funds.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Save the file as `index.html`.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, etc.).
3. Bookmark the page in your browser for fast daily access!

---

## ⚡ Features Overview

- **Main Balance & Funds:** Set your total available money or add new income directly.
- **Savings Buckets:** Allocate money into dedicated buckets (up to 10) with custom target goals and progress bars.
- **Fund Transfers:** Move money around freely between your main balance and savings buckets.
- **Expense Logging:** Quickly log spending by category with notes. Filter views by **Today** or **All History**.
- **Default Currency:** Pre-configured to **Philippine Peso (₱)**, with support for other currencies.
- **Dark / Light Mode:** Easy toggle in preferences that stays enabled even when data is reset.
- **CSV Data Export:** Backup your complete spent history to a spreadsheet file anytime.

---

## 🔒 Personal Data & Storage

- **100% Private:** Your financial details never leave your computer or web browser. No accounts or internet connection required.
- **Auto-Save:** Everything saves automatically to your browser's `localStorage`.
- **Backup Tip:** Download a CSV export from the **Settings & Actions** tab occasionally to keep a permanent record of your log history.
